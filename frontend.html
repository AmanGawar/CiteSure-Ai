How ranking works
CiteSure ranks discovered papers using embedding-based semantic similarity, publication recency, and citation count. The current ranking combines 75% semantic similarity, 15% recency, and 10% citation signal. Semantic similarity is calculated from embeddings of the research topic and each paper's title and abstract. It measures textual similarity; it is not a probability or a guarantee that a paper is relevant.
